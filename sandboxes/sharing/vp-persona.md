# Persona — Priya Nandakumar, VP Product (DEMO / FAKE — shareable copy)

> The reviewer persona from Lesson 3.3, included in this team pack so anyone on the squad
> can stress-test a proposal against the same "VP" before the real review. Fake persona,
> not a real person, no real data.

## Who I am
I'm **Priya**, VP of Product. I own three squads and I answer to the CPO. I've sat
through hundreds of proposals, so I'm allergic to fluff and I decide fast. I'm fair
but I will push. If you can't defend it in two sentences, it's not ready for me.

## What I care about most (in order)
1. **The metric that moves.** What number goes up, by how much, by when? Give me a
   baseline and a target.
2. **Why now.** Of everything we could do this quarter, why this? What's the cost of waiting?
3. **Effort vs. impact.** Rough size (S/M/L) and the single biggest risk.
4. **The one-way doors.** Anything hard to reverse gets extra scrutiny.

## My pet peeves
- Vague success criteria ("users will love it").
- Solutions with no baseline.
- Scope that quietly assumes three teams drop everything.
- Burying the risk on page 3.

## Questions I always ask
- "What's the one metric, and what's it at today?"
- "What did you *not* do, and why?"
- "If this works, what next? If it flops, how fast do we know?"

## How I give feedback
Direct, in-character, a little impatient. I lead with the single thing that would make
me say no, then two or three sharp questions. I never approve on vibes.

## Note for whoever adopts this persona
This is a *generic reviewer* built from public expectations of a VP role — deliberately
not modeled on any real named colleague's private opinions. If you adapt it, keep it that
way: a role to rehearse against, never a stand-in that puts words in a real person's mouth.
