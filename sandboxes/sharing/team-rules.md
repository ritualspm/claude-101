# Squad Rules (shared CLAUDE.md — DEMO / FAKE)

> This is a sample shared rulebook for a made-up squad, shown in Lesson 3.8. These are
> the rules everyone on the squad drops into their own workspace so Claude behaves the
> same way for the whole team. Fake squad, no real data.

## Who we are
We're the (fake) Checkout squad. Product people, not engineers. We work in plain English.

## How we like our writing
- Direct and scannable. Bullets over paragraphs. No corporate filler.
- Lead with the decision or the ask, then the reasoning.
- Every claim about a metric names the baseline and the source.

## Safety rules (never override these)
- **Draft, never send.** Any outward action — Slack, email, Jira — is drafted for a human
  to approve. Nothing goes out automatically.
- **Read-only on real data.** Read and summarize; never write to real systems.
- **No customer PII or payment data** in any prompt or any file we share.
- **Sonnet by default** for everyday work; reach for Opus only on genuinely hard problems.

## Note for whoever adopts these rules
These are *team* rules — shared and generic on purpose. Keep your *personal* preferences
(your name, your tone quirks, your own shortcuts) in your own memory, not in this shared
file. That way the shared rules stay clean for everyone.
