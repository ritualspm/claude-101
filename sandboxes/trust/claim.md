Here's the plain-English version of the Q3 rider peak-hour bonus:

- To qualify, a rider needs **at least 12 deliveries** in a single peak window
  **and** an acceptance rate of **80% or higher**.
- There are two peak windows: **Lunch** (Mon–Fri, 11:30–14:00) and **Dinner**
  (every day, 18:00–21:30). Weekend lunch does not count.
- Bonus tiers: **Standard** (12–19 deliveries) pays 40 SNK, **High** (20–29)
  pays 75 SNK, and **Elite** (30+) pays 120 SNK.
- Bonuses are paid out the **following week**. Riders have **14 days** from
  payout to raise a dispute.
- Riders in their first 30 days (probation) can't reach the Elite tier.
