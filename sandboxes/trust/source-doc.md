## 1. Peak-hour bonus eligibility

Riders qualify for the peak-hour bonus when **all** of the following hold:

- They complete **at least 12 deliveries** during a single peak window.
- Their **acceptance rate is 85% or higher** across the window.
- The peak window is one of the two defined below.

## 2. Defined peak windows

| Window | Days | Hours (local) |
|---|---|---|
| Lunch | Mon–Fri | 11:30–14:00 |
| Dinner | Every day | 18:00–21:30 |

Weekend lunch (Sat–Sun) does **not** count as a peak window.

## 3. Bonus amounts

| Tier | Deliveries in window | Bonus (SNK) |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 12–19 | 40 |
| High | 20–29 | 75 |
| Elite | 30+ | 120 |

## 4. Payout timing

Bonuses are calculated weekly and paid out with the **following week's** regular
earnings cycle. Disputes must be raised within **14 days** of the payout date.

## 5. Exclusions

- Cancelled-by-rider orders do not count toward the delivery total.
- Riders on probation (first 30 days) are not eligible for the Elite tier.
