# Customer Refund Policy — FoodFlow (FAKE DEMO DATA)

**Doc owner:** Care Ops · **Last updated:** 2026-04-12 · **Status:** Active

> This is fictional content for a training exercise. Not a real policy.

## Automatic refunds

A customer is eligible for a **full automatic refund** when:
- The order is cancelled **before** the restaurant accepts it.
- The rider is not assigned within **25 minutes** of the restaurant accepting.
- The order arrives **more than 40 minutes** later than the promised delivery time.

Automatic refunds are credited to the original payment method within 5 business days.

## Manual review refunds

For "item missing" or "item wrong" claims, an agent reviews the case. Partial refunds are issued at the item level. A customer may raise at most **3 manual claims per rolling 30 days** before the account is flagged for review.

## What is NOT refundable

- Dissatisfaction with taste or portion size (routed to the vendor, not refunded).
- Delays caused by an incorrect delivery address provided by the customer.
