# Rider Incentive Scheme — Q3 Pilot (FAKE DEMO DATA)

**Doc owner:** Logistics · **Last updated:** 2026-06-30 · **Status:** Pilot (3 cities)

> Fictional content for a training exercise. Not a real scheme.

## Peak-hour bonus

Riders earn a **peak-hour bonus of €1.50 per delivery** completed between 18:00 and 21:00 local time on Fridays, Saturdays, and Sundays.

## Streak bonus

Completing **10 consecutive deliveries** without declining a job pays a **€6 streak bonus**. Declining any offered job resets the streak counter to zero.

## Rain guarantee

When the weather feed reports active rain in the delivery zone, a **flat €0.80 per delivery** is added on top of any other bonus.

## Payout timing

All bonuses are settled in the **weekly rider payout**, every Tuesday, for the prior Monday–Sunday period.
