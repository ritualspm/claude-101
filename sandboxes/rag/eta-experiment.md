# Experiment Readout — ETA Padding A/B Test (FAKE DEMO DATA)

**Doc owner:** Product Analytics · **Last updated:** 2026-06-15 · **Status:** Concluded

> Fictional content for a training exercise. Not a real experiment.

## Hypothesis

Showing a slightly **more conservative (later) delivery ETA** will reduce "where is my order?" contacts without hurting conversion.

## Setup

- **Control:** current ETA model.
- **Variant:** ETA model + **4-minute padding** added to the displayed estimate.
- Run: 3 weeks, 2 mature markets.

## Results

- "Where is my order?" contacts **fell 11%** in the variant.
- Cart conversion moved **-0.3%** (not statistically significant).
- On-time delivery rate (delivered by the shown ETA) **rose from 78% to 91%**.

## Decision

**Ship the 4-minute padding.** The contact reduction and on-time lift outweigh the flat, non-significant conversion change. Revisit padding size in 6 months.
