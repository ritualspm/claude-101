# Idea brief — "Rider Shift Swap" mini-tool (FAKE DEMO — not a real product)

*All of this is invented for a training exercise. No real riders, no real data, nothing to ship.*

## The itch
Riders on our (pretend) demo fleet keep asking ops to swap shifts by DM. Ops copies it into a
spreadsheet by hand. It's slow and things get lost. I keep imagining a dead-simple screen where
a rider posts "I want to give away my Friday evening shift" and someone else can grab it.

I'm a PM. I can't build this. But I want something I can *click* to show my squad what I mean,
so the conversation stops being "imagine a screen where..." and becomes "look at this."

## What I picture (rough — I'm not attached to any of it)
- A single page. No login, nothing fancy.
- A list of shifts people want to give away: day, time slot, the zone (just a made-up label like "Zone A").
- A button on each one: **Grab this shift.** When clicked, it moves to a "Taken" list.
- A little form at the top to post a new shift you want to give away.
- Should look tidy enough that I'm not embarrassed to screenshot it into a Slack thread.

## Obviously-fake seed data to start with (invent more as needed)
- Fri 18:00–22:00 · Zone A · posted by "Rider 7734"
- Sat 12:00–16:00 · Zone C · posted by "Rider 2210"
- Sun 09:00–13:00 · Zone B · posted by "Rider 8890"

(Rider IDs are made-up numbers. There are NO names, phones, emails, or addresses here — and
there must not be. This is a throwaway prototype to make an idea visible, not a system.)

## What I do NOT need (yet)
- Real rider data. Real anything. It's a picture of an idea.
- It to actually save anything permanently, or connect to a real system.
- It to be "production". If this idea gets traction, engineering builds the real thing properly
  — with security, review, and real data handling. This is the napkin sketch, not the building.
