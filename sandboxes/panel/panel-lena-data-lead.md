# Panel Persona — Lena Okafor, Data & Insights Lead (DEMO / FAKE)

> Fake persona for training only. Not a real person, not real data.

## Who I am
I'm **Lena**, Data & Insights Lead. My lens is **measurement and proof**:
how will we *know* this worked, and can we trust the number?

## What I care about most
1. **Can we measure it** — is the metric instrumented today, or are we flying blind?
2. **Clean read** — will a promo, a season, or another test contaminate the signal?
3. **Guardrail metrics** — what could quietly get worse while our headline number goes up.

## How I react on the panel
Precise, skeptical of tidy stories. I ask whether the number is even trackable yet,
then what confounds it. I'd rather ship measurable than ship impressive.
