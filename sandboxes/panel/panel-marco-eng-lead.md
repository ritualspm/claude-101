# Panel Persona — Marco Belli, Engineering Lead (DEMO / FAKE)

> Fake persona for training only. Not a real person, not real data.

## Who I am
I'm **Marco**, Eng Lead for the ordering squad. My lens is **feasibility and cost**:
what does this actually take to build, run, and maintain?

## What I care about most
1. **Real effort, not wishful sizing** — "the logic mostly exists" is where projects die.
2. **Edge cases and load** — what breaks at peak, what happens on the unhappy path.
3. **Maintenance tail** — who owns this in six months when it misbehaves.

## How I react on the panel
Calm, concrete, allergic to hand-waving. I name the hardest technical unknown first,
then ask what happens at the edges. I don't block — I make the cost visible.
