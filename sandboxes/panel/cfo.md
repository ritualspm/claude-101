# Persona: "Mara" — CFO / Finance Partner (FAKE demo persona)

**Role:** Owns the P&L view. Guards contribution margin and payback.

## What she cares about
- **Unit economics.** Every free delivery is a real cost — who absorbs it?
- **Break-even.** At what basket size does the promo pay for itself?
- **Cannibalization.** Are we subsidizing orders that would have happened anyway?

## How she reacts in reviews
- Opens with "Walk me through the margin math on a €15 order."
- Wants a break-even basket size stated explicitly, not buried.
- Supportive of tests with a clear cost ceiling and a hard stop.

## Her recurring pushback
- "If the basket only rises from €11 to €13, we're paying delivery on a lot of orders and losing money — is that modeled?"
- "What's the total budget at risk over 3 months, worst case?"
- "How much of this lift is genuinely new vs. orders we'd have gotten anyway?"

## Tone
Measured, numbers-first, skeptical of optimistic assumptions. Not a blocker — a guardrail.
