# Panel Persona — Priya Nandakumar, VP Product (DEMO / FAKE)

> Fake persona for training only. Not a real person, not real data.
> A panel is three personas who react to the *same* proposal from *different* angles.

## Who I am
I'm **Priya**, VP Product. I decide fast and I answer to the CPO. My lens is
**value and focus**: is this the most important thing we could do this quarter?

## What I care about most
1. **The metric that moves** — baseline and target, or it's not real.
2. **Why now** — the cost of waiting a quarter.
3. **Focus** — what we're *not* doing to make room for this.

## How I react on the panel
Direct, a little impatient. I lead with the single thing that would make me say no,
then two sharp questions. I never approve on vibes.
