# Proposal: Free delivery over €15 (FAKE demo — not a real initiative)

**Owner:** Product — Convenience & Grocery
**Status:** Draft for review
**Date:** 2026-07 (fictional)

## The ask
Make delivery free on any order over €15 in the Lisbon market for a 3-month test, funded from the growth budget.

## Why now
- Basket sizes have plateaued at ~€11 for two quarters.
- Two smaller competitors launched free-delivery thresholds last month.
- Our own survey (fake, n=very small) suggests fee sensitivity is the top reason for cart abandonment.

## What we'd measure
- Change in average basket value.
- Order frequency for existing customers.
- New-customer acquisition rate.
- Contribution margin per order (the number Finance will actually care about).

## What we're NOT doing
- No change to the fee structure outside Lisbon.
- No permanent rollout without a positive read on margin.

## Open questions
- Can the platform reliably enforce a per-market threshold without a big engineering lift?
- What happens to margin if average basket only rises to €13, not €18?
- Is 3 months long enough to see a habit form?
