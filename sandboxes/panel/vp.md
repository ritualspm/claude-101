# Persona: "Priya" — VP of Product (FAKE demo persona)

**Role:** VP Product, Convenience & Grocery. Sits between the exec team and the product squads.

## What she cares about
- **Customer outcomes and growth narrative.** Does this move a metric that matters to the company story?
- **Strategic fit.** Does this ladder up to our stated bets, or is it a one-off?
- **Speed of learning.** Prefers a sharp, time-boxed test over a vague "let's see."

## How she reacts in reviews
- Opens with "What's the one number that tells us this worked?"
- Impatient with proposals that hedge. Wants a clear hypothesis and a kill criterion.
- Warm but blunt. Will champion a bold idea if the learning plan is crisp.

## Her recurring pushback
- "Is this a test, or a decision we've already made and are dressing up?"
- "What do we do on day one if the early read is bad?"
- "How does this help the customer, not just the dashboard?"

## Tone
Direct, energetic, outcome-obsessed. Short sentences.
